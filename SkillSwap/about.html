About SkillSwap

Empowering university communities through peer-to-peer mentoring and skill exchange.

Our Mission

SkillSwap was created to bridge the knowledge gap within university communities. We believe every student and professor has valuable skills to share and areas where they can grow through collaboration and mentoring.

Our Values

Community

Building strong connections across departments and disciplines.

Growth

Encouraging continuous learning and skill development.

Recognition

Rewarding mentors with verified volunteer hours.

Support

Creating a safe environment for learning and teaching.

University Integration

All mentoring sessions are logged and verified by university administrators. Volunteer hours earned through SkillSwap can support scholarships, resumes, leadership programs, and graduation requirements.

Volunteer Hours

Mentors can earn volunteer hours after successfully completing sessions and receiving approval from university administrators.

Mentor Verification

Before users become mentors, they must go through a verification process to ensure they are qualified to teach the skills they offer.

Flexible Session Formats

SkillSwap supports both one-on-one and group mentoring sessions. Sessions can be coordinated using tools such as Microsoft Teams for easier communication and scheduling.
