About SkillSwap

SkillSwap is a university platform that connects students and professors through skill-based mentoring sessions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning more collaborative by helping students request support and allowing verified mentors to share their knowledge.

The Problem

Many students need help in skills like programming, design, English speaking, and presentations, but they may not know who can help them.

Our Solution

SkillSwap allows students to post learning requests. Verified mentors who have the needed skill receive notifications and can accept the session.

Volunteer Hours

Mentors can earn volunteer hours after completing sessions and getting university approval.

Mentor Verification

Before users become mentors, they must be verified to make sure they are qualified to teach the skills they offer.
