How SkillSwap Works

SkillSwap makes it easy to request help, connect with mentors, schedule sessions, and track volunteer hours.

1. Create an Account

Students and professors register using their university information.

2. Become a Verified Mentor

Users who want to teach skills apply to become mentors. The university verifies them before they can accept sessions.

3. Post a Learning Request

A learner posts the skill they want to learn, session type, preferred time, and description.

4. Receive a Notification

Mentors with matching skills receive the request in their inbox and can accept or reject it.

5. Chat and Schedule

After a mentor accepts, both users can chat and choose a suitable session time using the schedule page.

6. Complete the Session

The session can happen online through Microsoft Teams or in person at the university.

7. Approve Volunteer Hours

After completion, the admin reviews the session and approves volunteer hours for the mentor.
